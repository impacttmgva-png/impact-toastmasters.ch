COME ALONG

Your first meeting
starts here.

An evening of prepared speeches, spontaneous ideas and constructive feedback.

What to expect

You don’t need to prepare a speech for your first visit. Come along, listen and get to know us. Impromptu speaking is an opportunity, not an obligation.

  1. Welcome & introductionsMeet the members and settle in.
  2. Prepared speechesHear ideas, stories and different perspectives.
  3. Impromptu speaking & feedbackDiscover Table Topics and constructive evaluation.
  4. Connection & conversationEnjoy a break and ask your questions.

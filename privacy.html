Privacy

This site does not include a contact form, advertising trackers or a member account system. Contact and event registration links take you to external services, which apply their own privacy policies. The hosting service may process information necessary to provide and secure the site.

Images and member information

Photography areas currently contain labelled placeholders. No member testimonials or committee identities have been invented. Club photographs and personal profiles can be added with the people concerned’s permission.

Meeting information

Always check the linked Meetup event before travelling. Published meeting schedules may change.

Contact

For questions about this site or club content, contact the organisers through the club’s Meetup page.

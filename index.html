A LITTLE COURAGE. A LOT OF SUPPORT.

You don’t have to be
a natural speaker.

You just need a place to begin.

Whether you’re finding the courage to speak up, preparing a presentation or learning to lead a room, you’ll find opportunities to practise here. Give it a try, receive constructive feedback and build on what you learn.

Your learning continues beyond our meetings. Through Toastmasters’ online learning platform, Pathways, you can explore specific aspects of public speaking you want to improve—from structuring a speech to using your voice and body language effectively. Our meetings give you the opportunity to put that theory into practice, receive constructive feedback and develop your skills step by step.
